1. THE BOOK (no matching yet)
-----------------------------
[ ] Two sides: bids and asks, each a set of price levels.
[ ] Price level = all resting orders at one price, in time priority
    (sequence number order). Front of the queue trades first.
[ ] add(order): only New/PartiallyFilled LIMIT orders rest. Insert at the
    back of its level; create the level if it doesn't exist.
    Market orders never rest (they're final after matching).
[ ] remove(order): take it out of its level; delete the level if it's now
    empty. Used by: Cancel, the old version on Replace, and fully filled
    orders during matching.
[ ] bestBid / bestAsk: the top level of each side, or "none" if empty.
[ ] Replace in the book (uses Order::replace's result):
      - old version: remove from the book.
      - new version, priority KEPT (same seq): takes the old version's
        place in the queue, not the back.
      - new version, priority LOST (fresh seq): back of its (possibly new)
        price level.
    Decide how to "take the old place" with your chosen queue structure.
[ ] Book-level queries for tests: number of levels per side, total quantity
    at a price, order IDs at a price in queue order.

Book invariants (write a checkBook helper, call it after every operation):
[ ] An order is in the book  <=>  its state is New or PartiallyFilled.
[ ] No empty price levels.
[ ] Within a level, sequence numbers strictly increase front to back.
[ ] Every order sits at the level matching its own price and side.
[ ] Not crossed: bestBid < bestAsk whenever both exist (holds trivially
    until matching exists, but check it from the start).


2. MATCHING
-----------
[ ] Crossing: an incoming order can trade if
      - buy limit:  price >= bestAsk
      - sell limit: price <= bestBid
      - market:     the opposite side is not empty
[ ] The match loop, for an incoming (aggressor) order:
      while it has remaining AND it crosses the best opposite level:
        - take the FRONT order of that level (the resting order)
        - trade qty   = min(aggressor remaining, resting remaining)
        - trade price = the RESTING order's price (price improvement goes to
                        the aggressor)
        - apply a fill to BOTH orders through Order::apply
          (PartialFill or FullFill: let fill qty vs remaining decide which;
          a Bug outcome here means a matcher bug -> fail-stop)
        - if the resting order is now Filled, remove it from the book
    then:
      - limit aggressor with remaining  -> rests in the book (add)
      - market aggressor with remaining -> MarketNoLiquidity -> Cancelled
[ ] Market buy walks asks from the lowest price up; market sell walks bids
    from the highest price down. Each level fully, front to back, before
    moving to the next price.
[ ] Record each trade (both order IDs, price, qty) for the execution
    reports and output log in step 3.

Matching invariants and tests:
[ ] After every incoming order: book not crossed.
[ ] Quantity conservation: per trade, both sides' filled go up by the same
    qty; across a whole test, total bought == total sold.
[ ] A market order is always final (Filled or Cancelled) after matching and
    never in the book.
[ ] Price-time priority: at one price, the earlier order (lower seq) fills
    first; a better price fills before a worse one regardless of time.
[ ] Scenarios to cover:
      - no cross: limit rests, book unchanged otherwise
      - exact match: both Filled, level removed
      - aggressor larger than one resting order: walks the queue
      - aggressor walks several price levels
      - aggressor partially fills then rests (limit)
      - market partially fills then Cancelled (no liquidity)
      - market into an empty side: New -> Cancelled
      - cancel / replace of a resting order, then matching respects the
        new queue position


3. ENGINE LOOP (preview, its own TODO later)
--------------------------------------------
- Read sequenced requests (NewOrder / Cancel / Replace) from an input log.
- For each: create or look up the order, run it through the book/matcher,
  write execution reports + book changes to an output log.
- Owns the sequence counter (fresh seqs) and the fail-stop abort on Bug.
- Determinism test: same input log twice -> byte-identical output.
